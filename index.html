Welcome to the clear world

This is the visual language of the mid-2000s — optimistic, liquid, reflective. Soft blues and greens, rising bubbles, floating cities inside crystal spheres, and interfaces that felt warm and alive.

Everything here is pure HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks. Just the feeling of opening a window into that era.
