What is Frutiger Aero?

Named after Adrian Frutiger and Windows Aero, this mid-2000s to early-2010s aesthetic celebrates optimistic technology meeting nature. Think glossy glass, soft blues & greens, bubbles, aurora lights, skeuomorphism, and that warm feeling of early internet hope.
