A world of water and light

In the Frutiger Aero imagination, the digital and the natural are not separate. Cities float inside transparent spheres. Fish swim past interfaces. Grass grows under glass. Light passes through everything.

This was the visual dream of a future where technology did not replace nature — it lived inside it, reflected it, and made it more beautiful.

Under the surface

Clear water, rising bubbles, soft caustics and underwater meadows full of light.

Inside the bubble

Entire cities and landscapes held inside perfect crystal spheres floating in the sky.

Open fields

Endless green hills under bright blue skies, butterflies and soft lens flares.

Friendly machines

Devices that feel warm, rounded and alive — part of the landscape rather than against it.

Stay in the clear blue

This is only the beginning of the aesthetic.
