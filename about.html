What is Frutiger Aero?

Frutiger Aero is the name given to the design language that dominated the mid-2000s to early 2010s. It is named after the type designer Adrian Frutiger and Microsoft’s Windows Aero interface.

It is defined by glossy and reflective surfaces, soft transparent glass, bright blues and greens, nature imagery mixed with technology, bubbles, water, soft light, and an overall feeling of optimism about the future.

Core characteristics

Glossy reflections Glass & transparency Water & bubbles Soft gradients Nature + technology Skeuomorphism Optimistic futurism Clear blue skies

You can see it in Windows Vista and 7, early iPhone and iPod interfaces, many stock photos of the era, game consoles like the Wii, and countless corporate websites and advertisements from that time.

Why it feels special

Unlike the flat design that followed, Frutiger Aero treated digital interfaces as physical objects that could catch light, hold water, and live inside a natural world. The result was warm, friendly and hopeful — a visual language that believed technology and nature could exist in perfect balance.
